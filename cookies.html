Cookie notice

No tracking. Minimal storage.

Trillion Euro sets no cookies, runs no advertising or cross-site tracking, and uses no third-party trackers. Visit statistics come from Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights, which are cookie-free and do not follow you across sites.

The site uses your browser’s local storage for two strictly necessary purposes — which is why no consent banner is shown:

ItemPurposeLifetime
mv.sessionKeeps you signed in on this device (your Supabase Auth session token).Until you sign out, or the session expires
mv.refRemembers an invitation code from a link you followed, so the person who invited you is credited if you join.30 days, this device only

Clearing your browser’s site data removes both. If we ever add anything beyond this, we will ask first.
