About & disclosure

One founder. In public.

TrillionEuro.com documents one entrepreneur’s attempt to build a €1 trillion personal fortune, and keeps a permanent record of the people who joined in.

Who runs this site

The operator’s legal name and address are published here, and on the contact page, before paid entries open.

Email: hello@trillioneuro.com · Privacy: privacy@trillioneuro.com

How this makes money — plainly

Founders’ Record entries are sold as a digital product. The entry fee is revenue of the founder’s business. It is not a donation, it does not go to charity, it is not tax-deductible, and it is not an investment in the founder or in any company. Buying an entry does not make the challenge more likely to succeed in any way that benefits you financially, and you have no claim on whatever the founder earns.

What the founder will and won’t claim

  • No invented participant counts, net-worth figures, press coverage or testimonials. Ever.
  • Wealth figures are published only with a date, source and method — see the methodology.
  • No suggestion of affiliation with, or endorsement by, Elon Musk or any of his companies.

Earlier free list

Before the Founders’ Record, this site kept a free, unpaid sign-up list. Those sign-ups are not paid entries and do not hold Founders’ Record numbers; the list is preserved separately at /early-record.
