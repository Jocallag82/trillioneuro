Honesty first

What this is — and isn’t.

A movement this ambitious only works if it is straight with you from day one.

What it is

Trillion Euro is a free, global community. Its mission is an ambition: for people everywhere to create €1 trillion in value over the long term and put it back into the world through initiatives chosen and shaped by the community.

Today, in Phase 1, we are building the community: registering founding contributors, forming country communities, voting on what to tackle first, and inviting others.

What it isn’t

  • Not a fund. The €1 trillion is a target, not money held, raised, pledged or guaranteed.
  • Not an investment, a donation drive or a crowdfunding campaign. Nothing here asks for or accepts payment.
  • Not a promise of impact. The roadmap is a set of future ambitions. Only Phase 1 has begun.
  • Not a pyramid scheme. There is no money in the system, so there are no rewards for inviting people.

What is real today

  • Every counter, ranking and percentage comes from real registrations and votes. If it is zero, it says zero.
  • Milestones are announced only when genuinely reached.
  • There are no invented testimonials, endorsements, statistics or contributor activity anywhere on the site.
  • The “arithmetic” facts on the homepage (for example, how long a trillion seconds lasts) are labelled as arithmetic to show scale, not as forecasts.

How decisions will be made

Priorities are voted on by contributors, one vote each. Turning priorities into initiatives, and building the legal and governance structures to deliver them transparently, are future phases — and will be done with proper professional advice and in public.
