    Questions

    Privacy, safety and the small print.

    Who can see what I post?

    Every confirmed member. There's no per-post audience setting yet. Nothing in the app is public on the web or indexed by search engines; community public pages show only a name, description and counts.

    Images are stored at long random addresses — anyone with an image's exact address can open it, so don't post images that must stay strictly private.

    How do muting, reporting and moderation work?

    Mute a person or a word and their posts disappear from your feed. Mutes and reports are private. Reports go to a moderation queue reviewed by a person, who can keep the content, remove it, or remove it and suspend the author.

    Blocking isn't built yet.

    How do I delete my account or data?

    Delete any post or reply yourself and it's removed from the database straight away. To delete your whole account, email privacy@ripestream.com — we act within 30 days. Self-service deletion is on the list.

    Do you use AI or sell my data?

    No AI ranks or summarises your feed, and we don't sell, rent or share your email. We use cookie-free page analytics to count visits. Data is stored with Supabase in the EU (Ireland). Full details in the privacy policy.

    What does Founding Member actually mean?

    A permanent, numbered record that you were one of the first 10,000 to confirm. It's free and can't be bought or transferred. It doesn't unlock paid features — there aren't any.

    I joined but lost my link.

    Go to the member page and enter your email — we'll send a fresh sign-in link (at most one every five minutes).
