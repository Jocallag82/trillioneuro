The idea

Social media has become a lot of things.

  • Following
  • Communities
  • Short video
  • Stories
  • Discussions
  • Messaging
  • Discovery
  • Local
  • Events
  • Creators
  • Groups
  • AI-powered discovery

Why shouldn't they work together?

Right now your social life is scattered. Friends in one app. Communities in another. Local news in a group chat. Events somewhere else entirely.

Imagine one place where it all connects — without turning into one giant, noisy feed.

RipeStream is being built to bring the best parts of modern social into one coherent experience. That's the goal. This is where we're heading.

Founding Members

Be here from the beginning.

The first 10,000 members become Founding Members. Not a tier you buy or a badge you grind for — just a permanent record that you were here first, and helped decide what this became.

  • Permanent Founding Member statusNumbered in the order members confirm, and kept for good — it doesn't lapse when the founding phase ends.
  • Early accessYou're in before RipeStream opens to anyone else.
  • A real voice in shaping itFounding Members are asked first about features, feed defaults and community rules.
  • The early communityA place alongside the other founders and the people building RipeStream.
  • Invitations for people you trust5 a month, to bring in the people you actually want here.

Then it's invite-only.

  1. 1
    10,000 founding places

    Claimed in the order people confirm their email. When the last one goes, founding membership closes for good.

  2. 2
    Every new member is invited

    After that, joining needs an invitation from someone already here. Everyone arrives knowing someone.

  3. 3
    5 invitations a month

    Each code works once, for one person, and expires after 30 days. No points, no rewards, no leaderboard.

No payment, no tokens, no waiting-list games. Founding status is recognition, not something for sale.
