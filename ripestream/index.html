Why RipeStream

You can see the algorithm.
And you can change it.

Every big platform ranks your feed. None of them show you how. On RipeStream, every recommended post carries a plain answer — "You joined Running Ireland", "Posted in Clondalkin, your area" — and one tap shows the full score and lets you turn it up, down or off.

Four feeds, not one black box

Following (chronological, no ranking), Communities, Local, Discover — or your own mix, e.g. 60 / 25 / 10 / 5.

More, less, never

Tune any topic, community or person. Mute without them knowing. Block in one tap.

Reset my feed

Wipe everything it learned about you and rebuild from your own choices. One button.

No dark patterns

No autoplay, no paid boosts in your feed, no "you might like" notifications, account deletion in two taps.

How it works

The whole formula, in public.

Posts score points for: you follow the author (35) · from a community you joined (25) · matches your interests (20) · near your area (15) · you've engaged with the author (15) · popular (10). Then freshness halves the score every 18 hours, and your "more / less" settings multiply it. That's it. No AI decides your feed and no AI writes posts.

Ireland first

Start local. Stay human.

RipeStream launches in Ireland with a small set of communities that actually have people in them — Running, Cars, GAA, League of Ireland, Tech, Parents, Food, and local ones for Dublin, Clondalkin, Tallaght, Lucan, Cork and Galway. Pick your area (never your exact location) and get local questions, events, clubs, lost & found and transport updates from people nearby.
