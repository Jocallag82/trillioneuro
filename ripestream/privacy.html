Privacy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

A plain-English summary of what we collect and why. RipeStream is in development; this page covers membership sign-up, founding places and invitations, and will be replaced with a full policy before the app launches.

What we collect

When you join, we store:

  • Your email address (required)
  • Your first name, if you choose to give it
  • Which form on the page you used, and when
  • When you confirmed your email, and your Founding Member number if you have one
  • If you joined with an invitation: which one, and who sent it
  • If you create invitations: their codes, the optional note you add (only you see it), and whether they've been used

Your member link is a random key. We keep only a one-way hash of it, so even we can't read it back.

We don't ask for a password, a phone number, payment details or your location. We don't set advertising or tracking cookies. We use cookie-free, first-party page analytics (Vercel Web Analytics) to count visits.

What we use it for

To run your membership — confirming your email, your founding place and your invitations — and to tell you about RipeStream: progress, previews and when there's something you can try. We don't sell, rent or share your email address with anyone for their own marketing.

Who can see what

If you join with someone's invitation, they can see your first name (if you gave one) and whether you've joined — nothing else, and never your email address. Nobody else can see that you're a member. The public site shows only the total number of founding places claimed.

Where it's stored

With Supabase (a hosted Postgres database) in the EU (Ireland). The tables holding your details cannot be read by the public website at all — the site can only call a small set of checked functions, and none of them return an email address.

How long we keep it

Until you ask us to remove it, or until we decide not to continue with RipeStream — in which case we delete the list.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about you at any time. Email privacy@ripestream.com and we'll act on it within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority — in Ireland, the Data Protection Commission.

Legal basis

Your consent, given when you submit the form. You can withdraw it at any time by asking us to remove you.
