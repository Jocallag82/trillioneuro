Designed around the learner

What you'll learn is up to you.

Red Teddy Tutor is being designed to support many subjects, learning goals and educational stages — with international curriculum support built up progressively over time.

  • SubjectsFrom sciences and maths to languages and humanities — the ambition is breadth, grown step by step.
  • GoalsUnderstanding a topic, practising for a test or preparing for exams.
  • StagesLearners of different ages and levels, from early learning onwards.
  • CurriculaAn international platform, with curriculum support developed progressively.

We won't support every subject, curriculum or age group at launch. Early access members will help shape where we start.
