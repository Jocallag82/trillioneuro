Learning games that are actually learning

Each game trains one skill from the school curriculum: times tables, number bonds, fractions or spelling. Rounds are short (about a minute or until you run out of lives), so they fit before school or after homework. The end screen shows what to practise next.

Safe for kids

  • No ads, chat, links or in-game purchases.
  • No account needed. Best scores are saved only on your device.
  • Works on iPad, Android, Chromebook and computers, with sound you can turn off.

Red Teddy’s adaptive lessons pick up where the games stop. They find what your child nearly knows, give hints instead of answers, and send parents a summary built from real answers. See the full K–12 curriculum.
