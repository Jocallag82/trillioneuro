How to play Fraction Pizza Shop

  1. Read the order, for example ¾ with tomato.
  2. Tap slices to add toppings. Tap a slice again to remove its topping.
  3. Press Serve. Fast, correct pizzas earn bonus points.
  4. In the Equivalent levels the pizza has more slices than the order: ½ of an 8-slice pizza means 4 slices.

Fractions you can see

Fractions confuse children when they are only symbols. A pizza shows what ¾ really means: the whole is cut into 4 equal parts, and you take 3 of them. The Equivalent levels teach the idea most children find hardest: ½, 2/4 and 4/8 are the same amount, shown on one pizza.

A wrong serve explains itself: "Not enough! 2/3 of 6 slices = 4". Every mistake becomes a lesson.

Skills and standards

  • Partition shapes into halves, thirds and fourths (Common Core 2.G.A.3)
  • Understand a fraction 1/b and a/b as parts of a whole (3.NF.A.1)
  • Recognise and generate equivalent fractions (3.NF.A.3, 4.NF.A.1)

Frequently asked questions

What age is the fraction pizza game for?

Ages 7 to 11 (grades 2–5). Halves and quarters suit grade 2. Equivalent fractions suit grades 3–5.

How do you teach equivalent fractions?

Show the same amount cut into different numbers of pieces. In this game ½ of a 4-slice pizza and ½ of an 8-slice pizza cover the same area.
