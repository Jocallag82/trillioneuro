How to play Spelling Bee Hive

  1. Pick a level: 3-letter words for K–1, up to long words for grade 4 and above.
  2. Listen to the word and look at the picture. Press "Hear again" whenever you like.
  3. Tap the letters in order. On a keyboard, you can type instead.
  4. After two wrong taps, the next letter glows green to help. Words spelled without a mistake score a Perfect bonus.

Spelling from sound, the way schools teach it

Children learn to spell by hearing a word, breaking it into sounds and matching each sound to letters. That is phonics in reverse. This game does exactly that: spoken word, picture clue, then letter by letter. Decoy letters make children think instead of guessing.

Skills and standards

  • Spell simple CVC words phonetically (Common Core L.K.2.D)
  • Spell words with blends, digraphs and vowel teams (L.1.2.D, L.2.2.D)
  • Spell grade-level multi-syllable words (L.3.2.E, L.4.2.D)

What age is this spelling game for?

Ages 5 to 10 (kindergarten to grade 4).

Does it read the words aloud?

Yes. It uses your device’s built-in voice. Press "Hear again" to repeat a word.

Is it free?
