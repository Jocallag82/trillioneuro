How to play Bubble Pop Bonds

  1. Choose a level: Make 10, Up to 20, Up to 100 (in tens) or Tricky 100.
  2. Tap one bubble, then tap another. If the two numbers add up to the target, both pop.
  3. Make three pairs to earn 3 extra seconds and a new target.
  4. Pairs in a row score bonus points. A wrong pair shows you its real sum, so every mistake teaches something.

Number bonds are the base of mental math

A child who instantly knows that 7 and 3 make 10 can solve 47 + 3, 70 + 30 and 10 − 7 without counting on fingers. Number bonds to 10 come first, then to 20, then multiples of ten to 100. This game trains that instant recall with no worksheets.

Skills and standards

  • Find the number that makes 10 when added to any number from 1 to 9 (Common Core K.OA.A.4)
  • Fluently add and subtract within 20 (2.OA.B.2)
  • Add within 100 using place value (1.NBT.C.4, 2.NBT.B.5)

Tips for parents

Make 10 is the most important level. Stay on it until your child finds pairs without counting, then move up. Saying the bond aloud ("six and four make ten") doubles the effect.

Frequently asked questions

What are number bonds?

Pairs of numbers that make a total, such as 6 + 4 = 10. Knowing them by heart makes addition and subtraction much faster.

What age is this for?

Ages 5 to 9 (kindergarten to grade 3).

Is it free?

Yes. It is completely free with no sign-up and no ads.
