A demonstration with fixed content. In the app, practice is generated for the student’s level; signed-in families can also ask Red Teddy for another explanation.

DemonstrationAlgebra I · Solving linear equations

Solve for x.

3(x − 4) = 2x + 5

  1. 3x − 12 = 2x + 5Expand the bracket: 3 × x and 3 × −4. The minus sign stays with the 4.
  2. x − 12 = 5Subtract 2x from both sides so x appears on one side only.
  3. x = 17Add 12 to both sides.
  4. 3(17 − 4) = 39 and 2(17) + 5 = 39 ✓Check by substituting back. Both sides match.

Subjects

Depth where it matters most.

Maths and English go deepest. Science, social studies and computer science are smaller and growing; the curriculum page lists exactly what exists at each grade.

  • Maths

    Ratios, algebra, geometry, trigonometry and derivatives, with computed answers re-checked independently.

  • English

    Grammar, figurative language, comprehension, literary analysis and argument.

  • Science

    Cells, photosynthesis, density, forces, chemistry and genetics.

  • Social studies

    US government and the Bill of Rights, history timelines, supply and demand, personal finance.

  • Computer science

    Reading and tracing Python: variables, loops and conditions.

  • Ask Red Teddy

    Topic-level tutoring for signed-in families: a simpler explanation, another example or a practice question, in a voice suited to older students.

How revision works

Study that plans itself.

  1. Find the gaps

    A short placement check per subject. If a foundation from an earlier grade is missing, the plan sends the student back to it.

  2. Practise what’s due

    Today’s plan mixes reviews, a mistake worth fixing and new topics whose prerequisites are in place.

  3. Review before it fades

    Secure topics return after 1, 3, 7, 14, 30 and 60 days. Shaky ones return sooner, easier first.

We don’t predict grades or promise exam results, and we have not mapped any exam board’s specification. Where a skill relates to a Common Core or NGSS standard, the curriculum page shows the code and whether coverage is full or partial.

Privacy & independence

Independent study, with parents in the loop.

Read the privacy notice

  • The tutor teaches; it doesn’t hand over answers. Replies that leak the answer are replaced with the written hint, and marking is always by fixed rules.
  • Messages to Red Teddy are not stored. Daily use is limited per family.
  • Parents hold the account. A student profile is a nickname and an age range.
  • Parent view separates what was measured from what is estimated, and progress can be deleted at any time.
