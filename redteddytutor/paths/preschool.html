Demonstration

Count the apples on the plate.

For the grown-up: if they guess, ask them to touch each apple as they say the number. Counting one-by-one is the skill, not getting it fast.

What they’ll explore

Five small skills that make school feel easier.

  • Numbers & counting

    Counting objects one by one, matching numbers to amounts, and comparing which group has more. Counting activities

  • First sounds & phonics

    Hearing the first sound in a word, rhyme, and early letter–sound links. Letter-sound activities

  • Shapes, colours & patterns

    Naming shapes, sorting by colour, and finishing simple patterns. Shape activities

  • Words & listening

    New vocabulary, following short instructions and talking about pictures. Rhyming activities

  • Play & problem-solving

    Memory pairs, matching and the Play Zone games for ages 1–5. Play Zone

  • Off-screen ideas

    Every age page includes real-world activities and the words to use. Pre-K readiness

How it works

Ten minutes, side by side.

  1. Pick their age

    Choose from ages 1–2, 2–3, 3–4 or 4–6 on the early-years page. No account, no details about your child.

  2. Play one short activity

    Instructions can be read aloud after a tap and are always shown as text. The question is carried by pictures wherever possible.

  3. Take it off-screen

    Each page suggests a real-world version (counting spoons, spotting shapes on a walk) so the screen is only the start.

Safety & parents

Designed for a grown-up nearby.

Read the privacy notice

  • No AI chat for preschoolers. Activities are pre-written. Ask Red Teddy, the AI helper, is only for signed-in families with children aged 5 and up.
  • No personal details. Nothing is asked about your child: no name, photo or birthday.
  • No messaging, profiles or feeds. There is nothing for a child to post and no one to talk to.
  • Sound only after a tap, every spoken line written on screen, and animation that respects reduced-motion settings.
  • No streaks or scores. A miss gets a gentle nudge, never a penalty.
