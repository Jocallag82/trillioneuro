How to read this page

Official standards
Codes such as 3.NF.A.1 or MS-LS1-6 belong to Common Core or NGSS. We link to the source and say whether our practice covers a standard fully or only in part. We never invent codes.
Our organization
Grades and course names are Red Teddy’s own way of grouping skills, following common US practice. They are not official.
Our teaching
Lessons, questions and explanations are original material written for Red Teddy Tutor.
State versions
Each state adopts and adapts standards in its own way. The national baseline is not any state’s curriculum, and no state alignment has been verified yet (see “States” below).
Review
Every skill passes automated checks (answer keys re-solved, prerequisites, duplicates, reachability). No skill has yet been reviewed by an outside qualified educator.
