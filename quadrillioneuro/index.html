Scenario 1

If AI’s appetite for electricity keeps growing…

Data centres used about 415 TWh in 2024 — around 1.5% of the world’s electricity. The IEA’s base case has that growing around 15% a year to 2030. What if growth is faster, slower, or lasts longer?

How to read this site

Are these forecasts?

No. A scenario is what follows if your assumptions hold. The sensitivity ranges show how quickly the answer moves when they don’t. Nobody knows these futures — including the organisations whose baseline figures we use.

Where do the facts come from?

Each fact card names its source and date: the IEA (energy, AI electricity, electric cars), the IMF (world GDP) and the UN (population). Where a figure is itself an estimate by that organisation, the card says so.

How is QuadrillionEuro different from TrillionEuro?

TrillionEuro is for building things: a scale ladder and unit economics for one idea. QuadrillionEuro is for thinking about the whole planet: technologies and markets at a size where they change energy systems and economies.

Is anything stored or sold?

No accounts, nothing stored on our side. Anonymous, cookie-free page analytics only.
