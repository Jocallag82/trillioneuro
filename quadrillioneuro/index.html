Questions, answered honestly.

Is this gambling?

No. Outcomes are decided entirely by who's willing to bid the most, not by chance — there's no randomness involved. It's an auction for a symbolic title, the same mechanic as any other auction house, just for a leaderboard position instead of an object.

Will I be charged anything by registering?

No. Registering interest is completely free and reserves your place in line for a seat. No payment method is collected and no bid is placed until bidding formally opens — which hasn't happened for any seat yet.

What if I register and never bid?

Nothing happens — there's no obligation. You'll simply be notified when your seat's bidding window opens, and it's entirely your call whether to take part.

What's the difference between this and Trillioneuro?

Trillioneuro is a free, permanent record of everyone who was here — join order never changes. Quadrillioneuro is the opposite mechanic: one seat per country (plus the World seat), won by whoever wants it most, and re-contested each season.

What happens if I lose a bidding season?

You're free to bid again next season. And if you've ever won a seat, that win stays in the permanent Hall of Champions record regardless of who holds the seat now — no past win is ever erased.

Why register before bidding even opens?

Each seat only opens for bidding once 1,000 people register interest in it — so registering now is what actually gets your seat closer to going live, and puts you in line ahead of people who register later.
