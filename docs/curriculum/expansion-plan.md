Activated: Precalculus (unit circle), Statistics (quartiles, IQR, outliers), World History,
English 11–12 (rhetoric). Every computed item is re-solved independently in
`scripts/expansion.test.mjs` (30,000+ items); authored banks are checked for one right answer.

No state-level alignment is claimed. Social studies, personal finance and computer science
skills have no mapped codes (standards vary by state; CSTA not yet mapped).

## Next, in priority order
1. **Math K–2 and grade 10 geometry**: K–1 measurement and time to the hour, grade 2 money,
   geometry proofs/congruence, similarity, coordinate geometry.
2. **ELA**: phonics beyond CVC (digraphs, vowel teams) for K–2; grades 7–12 reading
   (theme, author's purpose, informational argument) and writing conventions.
3. **Science K–4**: weather, plants and animals' needs, forces (push/pull), light and sound.
   High school: chemical reactions, ecology, waves.
4. **Social studies grade 7** (world geography/medieval) and **computer science** grades 3–6
   (sequencing, loops with blocks) and 11–12 (algorithms, data structures).
5. Outside educator review of all authored banks before claiming quality beyond tests.
