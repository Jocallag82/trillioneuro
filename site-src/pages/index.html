The mission

What if we could change the world together?

Trillion Euro is built on one extraordinary ambition: for people everywhere to create €1 trillion in value — and put it back into the world, through initiatives chosen and shaped by the people who join.

It is a long-term collective mission, not an existing fund. Today it is a community, and a community comes first: people, in every country, deciding together where to begin.

The €1 trillion is a target, not a balance. No money is being raised, pledged or held, and registration costs nothing.

Collective decision

The world has big problems. Where should we start?

Contributors choose what the movement should tackle first. One vote per contributor per round — and anyone can suggest a priority we have missed.

Voting helps establish the community’s priorities. It is not a binding financial commitment — no money is involved, and a vote doesn’t oblige you or the movement to anything.

The road ahead

Five phases. One beginning.

1

Build the global community Under way

Gather founding contributors from every country, form country communities and start talking. This is where we are today.

2

Establish worldwide priorities Future ambition

Turn the first votes into a clear, shared agenda — what the movement tackles first, and why.

3

Develop and evaluate actionable initiatives Future ambition

Work out what could actually be done about each priority, and test every idea honestly before it goes any further.

4

Establish transparent governance and operational structures Future ambition

Legal structures, public accountability and rules for how decisions are made — built in the open, with proper professional advice.

5

Begin delivering measurable real-world impact Future ambition

Put value back into the world — and show the results publicly.

These phases are future ambitions, not completed achievements. Only Phase 1 has begun. Later phases depend on the community, on law and on proper governance, and may change as we learn.
