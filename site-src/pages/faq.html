FAQ

Straight answers.

An ambition this big deserves plain language.

Is Trillion Euro a fund or an investment?

No. The €1 trillion figure is a long-term aspiration for the value a global community might create and return to the world. No money is raised, pledged, held or promised. Nothing on this site is an investment product, a donation appeal or financial advice.

Does it cost anything to join?

No. Registration is free and no payment is requested at any point. If that ever changed it would be announced openly and nobody would be charged without a clear, separate opt-in.

What is a Founding Contributor?

The first 10,000 people to confirm their email address are recorded as Founding Contributors, with their number. It is recognition for being early. It carries no financial rights, no equity and no entitlement to anything.

What does voting actually do?

Voting helps establish the community’s priorities — what the movement should tackle first. Each contributor has one vote per round. It is not a binding financial commitment, and it does not oblige you, or the movement, to spend or give anything.

Are the numbers on the site real?

Yes — every counter, ranking, percentage and milestone is computed from real registrations and votes in our database. If nobody has joined from a country, it shows zero. Milestones (100, 1,000, 10,000 contributors and so on) are only announced when they are genuinely reached. There are no invented testimonials, endorsements or activity.

Why are there contributor rankings by country?

To show the movement is global — not to start a contest. A country’s number is simply its share of one worldwide total, and every country shares the same agenda.

Is the invitation link a pyramid or multi-level scheme?

No. There is no money in the system, so there is nothing to earn, no commissions, no levels and no rewards for recruiting. Your link only credits people who joined through it, as a count on your own dashboard.

Who decides how the value is used?

That is exactly what the later phases of the roadmap are for: developing initiatives, then building transparent governance and structures — with proper professional and legal advice — before anything is delivered. Contributors’ priorities come first.

What happens to my data?

We collect your name, email and country to run your account and your country’s community. Your email is never public. You can download or permanently delete everything from your dashboard. Read the full privacy notice.
