FAQ

Straight answers.

Is this an investment?

No. You’re buying a digital product: a numbered, permanent public entry and a certificate. You get no shares, no interest, no profit share and no claim on anything the founder earns. If the challenge fails — which is the likely outcome — nothing is owed to you.

Is it a donation or for charity?

No. The fee is revenue of the founder’s business. It isn’t charitable and isn’t tax-deductible.

What exactly do I get?

A unique sequential entry number, a public page with your nickname, country, message and entry date, a downloadable and printable certificate with a QR code, a referral link, and a place in your country’s count.

How much does it cost?

The exact price, including any VAT, is shown before you pay. One payment, no subscription.

When is my number assigned?

Only after our server receives verified confirmation of the payment from Stripe. Numbers are sequential in the order payments are confirmed and are never reused — even if an entry is later refunded or removed.

Can I get a lower number by paying more?

No. There is one price and numbers go strictly in order of confirmed payment.

Do I need to use my real name?

No. A nickname is enough. Your real name is optional, private by default, and only shown if you tick a separate box. You can turn it off later from your management link.

What’s public and what isn’t?

Public: entry number, nickname, country, message, entry date, and — only if you choose — your real name and referral title. Private: your email, your real name (unless shown), and payment details, which stay with Stripe. See the privacy notice.

Will my entry show up in Google?

Only after a moderator approves it. Until then the page tells search engines not to index it.

Can I change or remove my entry?

Yes. Use the private management link you get after paying, or the request form. You can hide your real name or message at any time and ask for removal or erasure. Your entry number stays retired either way.

How do referrals work?

You get a personal link. When someone who followed it completes a verified paid entry, it counts toward recognition titles: Ally (1), Herald (3), Standard-Bearer (10), Vanguard (25), Legend (100). Titles are badges only — never cash, commission, discounts or financial reward. Self-referrals, duplicate visits and automated traffic don’t count. You can hide your referral title.

Can I get a refund?

See the refund policy. In short: if something went wrong (charged twice, charged with no entry), you get a full refund. A refunded entry is removed from the public record.

Is Elon Musk involved?

No. “Beat Elon” names the founder’s ambition. There’s no affiliation, endorsement or contact.

Why would anyone do this?

Because it’s a story worth being part of — a public attempt at something that almost certainly can’t be done, with a permanent record of who was there early. If that’s not worth the price to you, don’t buy one.
