The ground rules.

1. What Trillion Euro is

Trillion Euro is a free community movement whose long-term mission is to create €1 trillion in value and put it back into the world, through initiatives chosen by its contributors. The €1 trillion figure is an aspiration. It is not money held, raised, pledged or guaranteed, and nothing on this site is an offer of investment, a donation appeal, a loan or financial advice.

2. No payments

Registering and using the site is free. We do not request, accept or process payments, pledges or donations through this site.

3. Who can join

You must be 16 or over. Give accurate information, keep your password private, and keep one account per person.

4. Voting and ideas

  • Each contributor has one vote per voting round. Votes express community priorities and are not a binding financial commitment by you or by the movement.
  • You may suggest priorities. Ideas are reviewed before publication; we may edit for clarity, decline, or remove ideas that are spam, abusive, unlawful, contain links, or promote a product, person or organisation.
  • By submitting an idea you let us display it, without your name, on this site.
  • Attempting to manipulate votes, counts or referral figures (for example with multiple accounts or automation) is prohibited; affected accounts and votes may be removed.

5. Invitations

Your invitation link credits people who join through it as a count on your dashboard. There are no payments, commissions or rewards for inviting. Do not spam: only invite people who would reasonably welcome it.

6. Founding Contributor status

The first 10,000 confirmed contributors are recorded as Founding Contributors with a number. This is recognition only; it carries no financial or governance rights.

7. Acceptable use

Do not attack, overload or probe the site, scrape personal data, impersonate others, or use the site unlawfully.

8. Availability, changes and liability

The movement is in its first phase; features and the roadmap may change. We provide the site “as is”. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for indirect or consequential loss; nothing limits liability that cannot be limited by law, or your statutory consumer rights.

9. Ending your membership

You can delete your account at any time from your dashboard. We may suspend accounts that breach these terms.

10. Law

These terms are governed by the laws of Ireland, and the courts of Ireland have jurisdiction, without affecting any mandatory consumer rights you have in your country of residence.

11. Changes to these terms

We will date changes here and tell registered contributors about material ones by email.
