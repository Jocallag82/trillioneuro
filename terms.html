What you're getting

Founding membership reserves your name a permanent place on the Trillioneuro public record. It is free for your first year. Trillioneuro is a celebratory public record — it is not a charity, an investment, a security, or a financial product of any kind, and reserving a place creates no financial return, ownership stake, or entitlement of any kind.

After year one

A one-off €1 payment keeps your place permanently and marks you as a verified Founder. This is currently planned but not yet active — nothing is charged automatically, and you will be notified before any payment is requested.

What you agree to

  • The name, message, country, and (if approved) photo/logo you submit will appear publicly on the record.
  • You won't submit content that is abusive, illegal, or infringes someone else's rights. We may reject or remove submissions that do.
  • Rank on the record is based on join order and may later be affected by features described on the home page (e.g. contested top positions). Your permanent record entry is not affected by rank changes.

No guarantees

We aim to keep the record online indefinitely, but Trillioneuro is an early-stage project and we can't make absolute uptime or permanence guarantees beyond our best effort.
