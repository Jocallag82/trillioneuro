Privacy notice · version 2026-11-21

Your data, plainly.

We collect the least we can, publish none of it, and never sell it.

Who is responsible

The controller is the operator of TrillionEuro.com. For anything privacy-related, write to privacy@trillioneuro.com.

We do not collect payment details (there are no payments), build advertising profiles, infer your location, or make automated decisions with legal effects.

What is public

Only aggregates: how many contributors a country has, rankings, vote totals and milestones. Your name, email and individual votes are never shown publicly, in page metadata or in share previews. Your optional display name is shown only to someone who opens your personal invitation link. Ideas you suggest are shown (without your name) once approved.

Who processes it

  • Supabase — database and authentication, hosted in the EU (Ireland). Processor. It also sends the account-confirmation and password-reset emails.
  • Vercel — hosting and cookie-free analytics. Processor; transfers outside the EEA are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Your account data: until you delete your account.
  • Sign-ups that never confirm their email are not counted anywhere and are cleared periodically.
  • If you delete your account, your profile, votes, invitation records and unpublished ideas are erased and your login is removed. An idea that the community has already voted on stays, with no link to you. Totals update immediately.
  • Rate-limit data: hours.

Your rights

You can access, correct, port, restrict or erase your data, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdraw consent at any time. Most of it is self-service: from your dashboard you can edit your profile and marketing choice, download a copy of your data, and permanently delete your account. For anything else email us; we answer within one month. You can complain to the Data Protection Commission (Ireland) at dataprotection.ie.

Cookies and storage

No tracking cookies. See the cookie notice.

Age

You must be 16 or over to register. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We will date any change here and, for material changes, tell registered contributors by email.
