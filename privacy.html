Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 July 2026

This page is a plain-English summary of what we collect and why, written by the Trillioneuro team rather than a law firm. If you need this reviewed for formal compliance purposes, please have a qualified professional check it against current Irish/EU data protection law before relying on it.

What we collect

When you claim your place on the record, we ask for:

  • Your name or handle (shown publicly on the record)
  • Your email address (used only to confirm your place and contact you about it — never shown publicly)
  • An optional message and country (shown publicly)

If you sign up through a friend's referral link, we record that link's code alongside your entry so we can credit them — this never affects your own permanent place or number, and your referral code is never linked to your email or personal identity publicly.

If you type your email into the hero field before opening the claim form, we may save it as a pre-registration so you don't have to retype it — even if you don't complete your claim.

What we don't collect

We never ask for payment details, passwords, or government ID. Founding membership is free, and no financial information passes through this site.

How it's stored

Your information is stored with Supabase (a hosted Postgres database) under row-level security rules that prevent anyone — including us — from reading other founders' email addresses through the public record. Only your name, message, country, and rank are ever displayed publicly.

Your rights

You can ask us to correct or remove your entry at any time by emailing us. Because the record's whole premise is permanence, removal will delete your entry rather than editing it — we don't retroactively rewrite history.

Who is responsible for your data

Trillioneuro is a trading name registered in Ireland and is the data controller for the information described above.

How long we keep it

Your public entry is kept for as long as the record exists — that is the point of it. Your email address is kept for as long as your entry stands, and is deleted when you ask us to remove it. Email addresses captured as pre-registrations that never became entries are deleted after 12 months.

Who else processes it

  • Supabase — hosts the database (EU region, Ireland).
  • Vercel — serves this website and records anonymous, cookie-free page analytics.
  • ipapi.co — receives your IP address once, to pre-fill the country field on the claim form. Nothing else is sent and no result is stored against you.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone beyond the processors above.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request to access or delete your data: privacy@trillioneuro.com. We aim to reply within 30 days, as required.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.
