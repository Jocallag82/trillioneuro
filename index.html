Questions, answered plainly.

Is this investment advice?

No. TrillionEuro does arithmetic on assumptions you choose, alongside published facts. It does not predict outcomes or recommend investments.

Where do the facts come from?

From the organisation named on each card — the IMF, Eurostat, the CSO, the IEA and the UN. Each shows its date. When a figure is itself an estimate by that organisation, the card says so.

Do you store my analyses?

Saved analyses live in your own browser. Share links carry the assumptions in the URL. Nothing is sent to us.
