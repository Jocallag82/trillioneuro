# RateMe.ie — audit and implementation plan (Nov 2026)

Positioning: **Real experiences. Better choices.** Independent Irish business discovery and reputation platform.
Consumers free; businesses €49.99/yr founding membership (kept: no evidence supports a change or extra tiers).

## Audit — prioritised findings

| # | Severity | Finding | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | P0 trust/legal | Disputes auto-removed a review when the reviewer didn't reconfirm in 14 days. Silence was treated as proof of fraud; a business could remove honest negative reviews from people who missed one email. | **Fixed** — silence escalates to a moderator; review stays published |
| 2 | P0 | Appeals table existed but neither party could submit an appeal; granting an appeal always "published" regardless of who appealed. | **Fixed** — one appeal per party within 30 days; granting reverses the decision |
| 3 | P0 | Moderator dispute findings were sent to the business only. | **Fixed** — both parties get the written finding |
| 4 | P1 | Disputes had a reason but no evidence field; reviewer could only click "keep", not give their side or withdraw. | **Fixed** — evidence (business), statement + withdraw (reviewer), reminder email |
| 5 | P1 | Reviewer's manage link could override a *moderator* removal by reconfirming. | **Fixed** — restore only for legacy automatic removals; moderator removals go through appeal |
| 6 | P1 | Scores from 1–2 reviews looked as authoritative as scores from 50. | **Fixed** — volume/recency confidence indicator on profiles and cards (explicitly not an authenticity claim) |
| 7 | P1 SEO | County/category combinations were query strings (`/categories/x?county=y`), not indexable, and county pages linked to every category including empty ones. | **Fixed** — `/county/{county}/{category}` (indexed only with ≥3 real listings), 308 from query form, sitemap, counted internal links |
| 8 | P2 | Homepage had no location discovery and showed the product demo twice. | **Fixed** — county discovery, new positioning, duplicate demo removed |
| 9 | Copy | Homepage, how-it-works, review policy, privacy notice, for-business FAQ, guides, report page all described automatic removal. | **Fixed** |

Verified as already sound (no change): ratings computed by DB trigger from published reviews only; rating-blind
screening; membership excluded from every sort; "Highest rated" requires ≥5 reviews; test data flagged, labelled and
hidden in production; membership only from verified, idempotent Stripe webhooks; IDOR/RBAC covered by e2e tests;
structured data only with real ratings; thin pages noindexed.

## Deliberately not built (needs evidence or legal input first)
- **Review photographs** — needs storage, EXIF stripping, moderation queue and a DPIA (photos of people/premises are personal data). Not shipping a half-moderated upload.
- **Rating dimensions** — adds friction before review volume exists; revisit per-category once a vertical has ~20 reviews/business.
- **Consumer accounts / saved businesses** — strategy doc defers; anonymous email-confirmed reviews are the lower-friction path today.
- **Evidence file uploads** in disputes — text evidence only for now (same storage/GDPR reasons).

## Open legal questions (unchanged: need an Irish solicitor)
DSA notice-and-action wording, Defamation Act handling, Omnibus disclosure wording, retention of dispute statements.
No compliance claims are made on the site.

## Deployment checklist
1. Run `npm run db:migrate` against production (applies `006_fair_disputes.sql`; flags — does not republish — any reviews removed under the old rule).
2. Deploy the `rateme` Vercel project.
3. In `/admin/reviews?status=escalated`, work through escalated disputes; check legacy auto-removals in the audit trail.

## Tests
Unit/integration (vitest, real Postgres): 50 passing, including the new dispute workflow and confidence rules.
E2E (Playwright, desktop + mobile, a11y, links, SEO): 39 passing, dispute specs rewritten for the fair workflow.
